About Us

A Southall Glazing Team, Working Across West London

We are S&K Double Glazing, based on Wentworth Road in Southall. We fit windows, doors and skylights on houses all over this part of West London, from bay windows on the older semis to full house replacements and flat roof lanterns.

We quote in plain numbers before any work starts. What we say on the quote is what you pay.
